      03 · TecHacks FX · Official Chrome extension · Free
      
        
      Take the launchpad into every call.
      
        
      A free Chrome extension for Google Meet. Your TecHacks background switches on by itself, with no green screen. You also get a crew badge, a live launch countdown and gesture effects. Everyone on the call sees it.
      
        
      
        
      Freev1.2 · 11 MBChrome · Edge · Brave · ArcRuns on your laptop
      
      
      
      
      
        
      MEET · TH-05 CREW SYNC
      
        
        
      
      
      
    
      
  
      

  
      
    
      What it does
      Six ways to show up on mission.
      Here the Pilot is on the call. On yours, it will be you.
      
    
      
      
      🌌 Auto background
      Your room, swapped for orbit
      Four TecHacks scenes, or blur, applied automatically. It cuts you out sharply, with no green screen.
      
      
      ⏱ Badge + countdown
      Your name, your track, the clock
      Your crew badge sits in the bottom-left corner. The countdown counts down the days to launch, then shows a live T+ clock during the 24 hours.
      
      
      👍 Thumbs up
      Ship it
      Hold a thumbs up for half a second. The Pilot’s rocket launches across your video and stamps it SHIPPED ✓.
      
      
      ✌️ Peace sign
      Hi, crew!
      Flash a peace sign and the Pilot pops up next to you to say hi. Perfect for the start of a stand-up.
      
      
      🙌 Both hands up
      Sticker rain
      Raise both open hands for a moment and TecHacks stickers rain down. Use it when your crew’s build finally works.
      
      
      🫰 Snap
      Vanish into orbit
      Snap your fingers and you turn to dust, leaving only the TecHacks background. Snap again to come back.
      
    
      

    
      Backgrounds
      Pick your launch site.
      Switch any time from the extension, or press Alt+Shift+B during a call.
      
    
      
      
      
      
      
    
      

    
      Install · 2 minutes
      Five steps to lift-off.
      
    
        
      
      1. Download and unzip
        Get TecHacks-5.0-FX.zip and unzip it somewhere permanent, like Documents. Keep the folder after installing.
        2. 
      
      2. Open extensions
        In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode (top right).
        3. 
      
      3. Load unpacked
        Click Load unpacked and choose the techacks-fx folder, the one with manifest.json inside.
        4. 
      
      4. Pin and set your badge
        Pin it from the puzzle-piece menu. Click the 5 icon to pick a background, then type your name, role and track.
        5. 
      
      5. Turn off Meet’s effects
        In Meet, open Apply visual effects and choose No effect. Reload any open Meet tab. You’re live.
        6. 
    
      
    
      ⚠️
      Meet’s own background runs after ours and covers it, so set Meet’s visual effects to No effect. If you installed Snap & Cinema FX before, switch it off.
      

    
      
      
      
        
      Gestures & shortcuts
      
        
      
          
      Do thisGet thisShortcut
      
          
            
      👍 Hold a thumbs upRocket launch + SHIPPED ✓Alt+Shift+T
      
            
      ✌️ Peace signThe Pilot says “Hi, crew!”Alt+Shift+H
      
            
      🙌 Both hands upTecHacks sticker rainAlt+Shift+M
      
            
      🫰 SnapVanish (snap again to return)Alt+Shift+S
      
            
      Next backgroundOrbit → Launchpad → Mission control → File room → Blur → OffAlt+Shift+B
      
                
        
      
        
      Click inside the call tab first so the shortcuts reach it.
      
      
      
      
      
        
      Good to know
      
        
      🔒
      Private by design. The AI runs on your laptop. No video leaves your computer except through the call itself.
      
        
      🪞
      Reads the right way round. Meet mirrors your self-view. TecHacks FX flips it back, so your badge isn’t backwards for you either.
      
        
      ⚡
      Light on your laptop. It uses only two small models. If an older laptop struggles, pick My room in the extension.
      
        
      🌐
      Not just Meet. It also works in Zoom, Teams and Discord in the browser.
